# Simulation and Optimization — Lecture Notes

These are the lecture notes for the simulation and optimization part of the course,
compiled from *An Introduction to Business Analytics* by Ger Koole (2019) per the
course schedule.

Each notebook follows the storyline of its lecture slides, at more depth and its own
pace, and ends with a **References** section listing the book section(s) it draws on
and any errata applied. The worked examples in the notes are deliberately *different*
from the ones used in the lectures. Use the menu on the left: click a lecture to expand
its list of topic notebooks.
